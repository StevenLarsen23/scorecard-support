Writing in

Every message goes to one person, and replies usually come within a few days. The fastest way to get a useful answer is to say which device you are on, which game you were scoring, and what you expected to happen instead.

The app can fill most of that in for you: open Contact Us on the home screen and choose Report a Bug or Request a Game. That opens a pre-addressed draft in your own mail app with the version numbers already filled in — nothing is sent until you tap send.
